## B. Finalist 1 — Ubiquinol

**SKU:** Doctor's Best Ubiquinol with Kaneka, 100 mg, 60 Softgels  
**Why selected:** Official facts state **Ubiquinol (Kaneka, reduced form of CoQ10) 100 mg** per softgel. Oil base, gelatin, soy-free, no PQQ/omega stack. Strongest ubiquinol label among in-scope brands with a usable 2026-09-16 brand PDP.  
**Bucket:** Ubiquinol.  
**Primary keyword opportunity:** supporting `best ubiquinol supplement` + branded `doctors best ubiquinol` on the product URL; hub still owns `best coq10 supplement`.  
**Label-source quality:** High (official facts 2026-09-16).  
**Testing evidence:** **unknown** — do not imply USP/NSF.  
**Price per 100 mg:** $0.62 ($36.99 ÷ 60, brand, 2026-09-16).  
**Claim risks:** Heart/energy/aging-adult brand copy; Kaneka seals. Mirror as `brand_claim`.  
**Remaining gaps:** Brand page **Sold out**; confirm a buyable lot and photograph if packaging differs.  
**YAML-ready?** **Almost** — facts are sufficient for a draft YAML **after** in-stock confirmation. Not ready to publish.

**YAML-ready?** **Almost** — same in-stock caveat as finalist 1.

## D. Finalist 3 — Budget / value

**SKU:** Nutricost CoQ10 200 mg, 60 vegetarian capsules (UPC 810014671659)  
**Why selected:** Lowest dated non-Amazon $/100 mg in this pass ($0.13 at $15.95 Vitacost). Simple single-active dry capsule vs oil softgels — useful comparison contrast.  
**Bucket:** Budget / 200 mg.  
**Primary keyword opportunity:** `nutricost coq10`; value narrative on compare table.  
**Label-source quality:** **Weak** — retailer panels only; no official nutricost.com PDP. Target vs Vitacost other-ingredients disagree (rice flour + hypromellose vs added stearate/silica).  
**Testing evidence:** **claimed** only.  
**Price per 100 mg:** $0.13 (Vitacost $15.95, 2026-09-16). Not Amazon.  
**Claim risks:** Retail titles using “high absorption”; retailer condition/heart copy — do not import.  
**Remaining gaps:** **Form word (ubiquinone vs unspecified)**; official facts; one other-ingredients list.  
**YAML-ready?** **No.** Checklist says reject if form or CoQ10 amount cannot be verified. Amount is on retailer facts; form is not. Capture a bottle panel or brand PDP first.
